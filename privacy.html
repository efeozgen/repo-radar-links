Privacy Policy

Faceless Studio · last updated November 25, 2026

Faceless Studio is a private publishing tool. It is used only by its operator to upload short videos to the operator's own channels (Repo Radar, World Brief and related channels). It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

When the operator connects their own YouTube account, the app requests these Google permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the operator's channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to post the first comment, with source links, under the operator's own videos.

The app does not read, collect or store data about viewers, commenters or any other Google user.

How data is stored

The access tokens Google issues are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local file that is never shared or uploaded anywhere. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or for training AI models.

Google API Services

Faceless Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Repo Radar channel's About page on YouTube.
